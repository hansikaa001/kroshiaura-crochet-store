It started with one hook and a ball of yarn.

kroshiaura began as a quiet hobby: a cosy corner, a favourite playlist, and a tangle of pastel yarn. One little flower turned into a whole bouquet, and friends started asking where they could get one.

Today every piece is still made by hand, slowly and with care. Our name comes from kroshi (crochet) and aura (the happy glow a handmade gift carries).

Every stitch holds a tiny bit of calm. I hope you feel it when you hold your piece.

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp anytime.

Are your products really handmade?

Yes! Every piece is crocheted by hand, so tiny differences make each one unique.

How long will my order take?

Since everything is made with care, time depends on the product. Ready items ship sooner, and made-to-order pieces take longer. We confirm the timeline when you order.

Can I get a custom color or design?

Of course. Message us with your idea and we will tell you the price and time. An advance is needed to start the work.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

No, we do not offer COD. Payment is via UPI or bank transfer.

Do you deliver all over India?

Yes, we ship pan India. Shipping is free on orders of ₹599 and above, and a flat ₹99 on smaller orders.

Can I return or exchange my order?

As items are handmade, we do not accept returns or exchanges. If your order arrives damaged, message us with an unboxing video within 24 hours and we will help. Advance for custom orders is non-refundable.

Will the product look exactly like the photos?

We try our best! Handmade pieces and screen settings can cause slight differences in color and size.

How do I take care of crochet items?

Spot clean or hand wash gently in cold water with mild soap. Do not wring or bleach, and dry flat in the shade.
